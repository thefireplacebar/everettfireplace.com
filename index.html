Everett's oldest running bar

Bars come and go. The Fireplace has stayed lit since 1940 — through booms, busts, and everything Everett has thrown at it. No gimmicks, no rebrand, no craft-cocktail pivot. Just a proper neighborhood dive that knows exactly what it is.

House rules are simple: no politics or religion at the bar, everybody gets a fair pour, and everyone's welcome — and we mean everyone.

Come for a beer. Stay for the next 85 years.
